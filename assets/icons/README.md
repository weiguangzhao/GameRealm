# Resource brand icons

These SVG assets identify the project's resource destinations and are served locally.
Brand marks belong to their respective owners and are not covered by any project license.

- `github.svg`: black Invertocat from the [GitHub Brand Toolkit](https://brand.github.com/foundations/logo), downloaded from its official `GitHub_Logos.zip` archive.
- `arxiv.svg`: small logomark from the [arXiv logo library](https://info.arxiv.org/brand/logos.html).
- `huggingface.svg`: color logo from [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/brand), served by its official `front/assets/huggingface_logo-noborder.svg` asset. A viewBox was added for responsive scaling; the artwork is unchanged.
